# Taste for decks

Decks get the largest creativity budget. A slide is a composition, not a page of text: the narrative and one strong visual idea per slide are the craft.

## A deck can be

A sequence of evidence ending in one decision; a short visual essay; one diagram that develops across slides; image and argument alternating; a briefing book meant to be read; a comparison held side by side the whole way.

## Composition moves (each has a job)

- **Type poster:** one sentence set huge; for a claim the audience must remember.
- **Full-bleed number:** one figure fills the slide; only when that number is the story.
- **Asymmetric split:** a narrow column of argument against a wide field of evidence.
- **Rail or sequence:** a repeated marker that shows where the story is (stages, stops, rooms).
- **Developing diagram:** the same drawing on several slides, one part added each time.
- **Side by side:** two options or two periods on the same scale.
- **Quiet text slide:** a paragraph with room around it, after loud slides.
- **Table as the slide:** when the comparison is the content.

## Your deck attractors

A dark opening slide; a small label plus a sentence title on every slide; a giant number beside a small stack; three numbered columns; a bar chart with a callout beside it. Use each at most once per deck unless the subject itself repeats.

## Dials on a slide

- Shape: `borderRadius` from 0 to about 24pt; pills are radius equal to half the height.
- Line: 0.5pt hairlines to 4pt bars; frames are `border`, single edges are `borderTop` / `borderInlineStart`.
- Scale: body 14 to 20pt; display from 40pt to 120pt or more for a poster slide.
- Ground: `--bg` and `d.slide({dark: true})` per slide; a dark ground is a rhythm choice, not a default opener.
- Density: 1 idea per slide (airy) to a full analytical slide; neither leaves the lower third empty by accident.

## Craft floor

Titles 28pt or more, body 14pt or more, labels 12pt or more; small text keeps contrast (the kit warns). Native charts and tables. No clipped text. Eight or more slides need a way to navigate (an agenda, a rail, a section marker).
